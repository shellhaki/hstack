## What this taught us about writing the guides
1. **A rule in a guide the model doesn't open does nothing.** At size S the model reads ROUTES,
   testing and verify only. Rules that matter on every task must live there.
2. **A general rule loses to in-the-moment reasoning.** "Don't narrow inputs" was read and then
   argued away ("integers keep the math exact"). The same rule written into the step where the model
   lists its test cases ("a `number` gets 0, a fraction and a very large value; each expects it to
   work") changed the code.
3. **Name the exact action.** "Never write to /tmp" failed three rounds; "back up with
   `cp <file> .hstack/work/tmp/`" inside the step that makes the backup worked first time.
4. **Still open:** truncate keeps a space before `…`, even though both the verify trap list
   (`trimEnd()`) and the testing case list ("a space at the cut point") name it. The model writes the
   case but expects the space to stay: the rule says what to test, not what the right answer is.
   Next: state the expected result in the case list ("`hello world` cut at 7 → `hello…`").
   `max 0` returning "" instead of throwing is a judgement call in the hidden test; leave it.
