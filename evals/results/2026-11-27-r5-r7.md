## Why checkout and events kept failing
Both were sized M. M follows ROUTES.md, and the trap table (with "2.5 seats is rejected" and "epoch
seconds and ms") lived inside the S card section of SKILL.md, so M runs skipped it. Fix for round 7:
the trap table is its own section that applies at every size; `think/verify` step 5 and a new
non-negotiable in ROUTES.md point at it.

## Lessons for writing guides (cumulative)
1. A rule in a file the model doesn't open does nothing.
2. A general rule loses to in-the-moment reasoning; the same rule at the step where the action
   happens works.
3. Give the right answer next to the rule (a worked example), with values that differ from any test.
4. Rules must say which sizes they apply to; anything filed under "S" is skipped by M and L.
5. Even then, some habits resist: writing scratch files to /tmp, and "the type is number so 1.5 is
   fine". They drop but don't vanish.

## Rounds 8–9: cutting cost on size S (due-date, rate-limit, slugify, duration; hstack only)
| Round | Change | Avg tokens | Avg tool calls | Hidden tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r6 | S card inside SKILL.md | 54.3k | 9.5 | 26/29 |

| plain (r1/r6) | — | ~39k | 3–8 | 24/29 |

What we learned:
- Token use tracks tool calls, not guide size: every call resends the conversation. The one run that
  used 4 calls cost 45k; runs with 11–15 calls cost 55–58k.
- Haiku ignores "open nothing else" and call budgets about 3 times in 4: it still opens ROUTES.md
  and 2–4 guides, and makes 9–15 calls. Instructions alone don't move the cost much further.
- What remains is ~1.35x plain Haiku, for 27/29 vs 24/29 on these tasks and 1.5–2x the tests.
