# Synthesis (frontier)

You write `REPORT.md` from the notes only. Your output is the expensive part, so the report is short
and every sentence is a conclusion or a decision, not a summary of the notes.

1. **Answer first.** The decision in one or two lines: build, don't build, choose X, wait until Y.
2. **Compare options** in one table: the options as rows, the criteria from the brief as columns,
   each cell short and tied to a note's source.
3. **Recommend** one option, with confidence (high, medium or low) and the 2 or 3 reasons that carry
   it. Say what would change your mind.
4. **Risks:** the few that matter, each with how to reduce it.
5. **Unverified:** what the notes couldn't confirm. Don't hide gaps in confident prose.
6. **Next steps:** concrete and ordered, written so the first one can become an `hstack` brief
   (WHY, WHAT, CONSTRAINTS) without rework.

Keep claims at the strength the notes give them: an estimate stays an estimate, one review is an
opinion, a gap with one source is "possible". Cite by note and source number, for example `[q2:4]`.
